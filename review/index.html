What to do, in order

1Trigger the follow-up sequence on lead arrival instead of on a manual intake-form send. It currently starts only when one person acts, and never at weekends.JLOne setting
2Turn on Smith.ai call transfer and intake completion. Both are already paid for and unused, and they convert the handoff from a note into a live conversation.MarkConfig
3Assign every new lead to a named person on arrival, with a same-day callback target. Two people make the great majority of calls with no queue feeding them.MarkProcess
4Add a cancelled and no-show branch back into the follow-up sequence.JLOne branch
5Hold consultations inside 72 hours. The current wait is what drives the cancellations.Richard / NickScheduling

One thing to hold. Paid search is above target and losing roughly half its available impression share to budget. Do not increase it yet: with three quarters of leads never called, more volume raises cost per client rather than lowering it. Revisit once items 1 to 3 are done.
