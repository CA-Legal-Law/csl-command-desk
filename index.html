Real demand

calls + forms actually received — independent of any ad platform's counting

⚠ Measured leads fell 59% after May — mostly self-inflicted, and recoverable

CallRail counted 146 leads in April, 34 by June. Splitting that by source separates two different problems. Website-pool calls fell 35 → 17 (about half, tracking the budget cut and the relaunch) — that part is a real decline in people calling. Google Ads call-extension calls fell 76 → 0 — that is not demand, it is plumbing: the CallRail→Google Ads integration failed and those calls stopped being placed and tracked. It was repaired 2026-08-20 and should recover. Forms held throughout and hit a yearly high in August. Because half a matter's cash arrives after day 90, collections have held up so far and the shortfall still lands roughly October 2026 onward — but it is smaller than the headline 59%, and the causes are ours to fix rather than the market's.
